8. PROFIT / LOSS ACCOUNTING
==================================================

Create a deterministic stake model.

Do NOT confuse:

For decimal odds O:

FULL WIN:

profit = stake × (O - 1)

FULL LOSS:

profit = -stake

PUSH:

profit = 0

HALF WIN:

profit = stake × (O - 1) / 2

HALF LOSS:

profit = -stake / 2

VOID:
